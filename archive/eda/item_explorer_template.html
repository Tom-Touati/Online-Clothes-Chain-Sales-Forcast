Four-month rolling averages, so forecasts and actuals compare like for like. The dark line is the actual: at each month, the average of that month and the three before. Each model's line joins its past forecasts: a forecast made with data through one month covers the next four and is plotted at the last of them as its total ÷ 4, so where it meets the dark line it was right. 13 backtest origins, data through Feb 2025 to Feb 2026; the scoreboard's "recent" score uses the 9 from Jun 2025. The point at Oct 2026 is the submitted Jul–Oct projection ÷ 4. Dashed lines continue the rolling average through Jul–Oct 2026 with each selected model's own projected months (blue = submitted).
