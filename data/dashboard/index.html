What the scores mean, and how this page is updated
WAPE ÷ R3
Total absolute error over all item-origins, divided by R3's. Big items weigh most.
Typical item ÷ R3
Geometric mean over items of (|error| + 1) ÷ (|R3 error| + 1): how the system does on an ordinary item, whatever its size.
Wins
Share of item-origins where the system's absolute error is smaller (ties count half).
(MAE + |bias|) ÷ R3
Per origin, total absolute error plus the absolute total bias, divided by actual units, averaged over origins; bias hurts as much as noise.
Pinball ÷ R3
Quantile loss at P10, P25, P50, P75 and P90, with every system carrying the production quantile model's spread (×1.25) around its own forecast.
Bias
Total forecast ÷ total actual − 1. Negative means under-forecast.
Buying loss
Profit lost against perfect foresight when the production buying policy orders from the system's monthly forecast.
95% intervals
Item bootstrap within origins (300 resamples) of the difference in WAPE ÷ R3 between the system and a reference. An interval entirely below zero means the system is reliably better.
Champions
The accuracy champion has the lowest WAPE ÷ R3 on the nine recent origins; the buying champion has the lowest buying loss among simulated systems. Reference rows (R3 and R3 + rules) cannot be champion.
Status
Where each system stands: a reference, the source of the submitted workbook, the basis of a candidate workbook, tested and not adopted, or new and not yet simulated.
Updates
When a new model is scored, the project script eda/159_scoreboard.py is rerun and its output replaces this page's data. Each system's decision id points to its entry in the project's decision log.
